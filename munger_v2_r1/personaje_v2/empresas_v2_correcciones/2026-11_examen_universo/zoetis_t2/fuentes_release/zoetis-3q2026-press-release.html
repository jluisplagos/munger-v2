Zoetis Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results; Highlights Progress Advancing Innovation Across Diversified Portfolio and Narrows Full Year 2026 Revenue Guidance, Raises Full Year 2026 Adjusted EPS Guidance

11/05/2026

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

  • Revenue: $2.4 billion, decreased 1% reported and organic operational1
    • U.S. segment revenue: $1.2 billion, decreased 6% reported and organic operational
    • International segment revenue: $1.1 billion, increased 4% reported and organic operational basis
  • Net Income: $591 million, decreased 18% reported
  • Adjusted net income: $720 million, decreased 5% reported and 7% organic operational
  • Diluted EPS: $1.44, decreased 12% reported
  • Adjusted diluted EPS: $1.75, increased 3% reported and 1% organic operational

PARSIPPANY, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Zoetis Inc. (NYSE:ZTS), the world's leading animal health company, today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

"Our third-quarter performance was in line with our expectations. Continued pressure in Companion Animal was partially offset by growth in Livestock and Diagnostics, reflecting the breadth of our portfolio," said Kristin Peck, Chief Executive Officer of Zoetis. "We remain focused on defending our position and directing our resources where they can have the greatest impact. And with the scale, expertise, and pipeline to keep setting the pace of innovation in animal health, we are confident in our ability to create long-term value for customers and shareholders."

THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
The company reported revenue of $2.4 billion for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 1% on a reported and an organic operational1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $591 million, decreasing 18% on a reported basis. Diluted EPS was $1.44, decreasing 12% on a reported basis.

Adjusted net income2 for the third quarter of 2026 was $720 million, decreasing 5% on a reported basis and 7% on an organic operational basis. Adjusted diluted EPS was $1.75, increasing 3% on a reported basis and 1% on an organic operational basis. Adjusted net income for the third quarter of 2026 excludes the net impact of $129 million for purchase accounting adjustments, acquisition and divestiture-related costs and certain significant items.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 SEGMENT HIGHLIGHTS
Zoetis organizes and manages its commercial operations across two segments: United States (U.S.) and International. Within these segments, the company delivers a diverse portfolio of products for companion animals and livestock, tailored to local trends and customer needs. In the third quarter of 2026:

  • Revenue in the U.S. segment was $1.2 billion, decreasing 6% on both a reported and an organic operational basis relative to the third quarter of 2025. Companion animal revenue decreased 8%, reflecting macro-driven price sensitivity and competitive pressure in dermatology, as well as generic competition. Livestock revenue increased 5% on both a reported and organic operational basis in the quarter, supported by strength across cattle, swine and poultry.
  • Revenue in the International segment was $1.1 billion, increasing 4% on both a reported and an organic operational basis compared with the third quarter of 2025. Companion animal revenue increased 1% on a reported basis and remained flat on an organic operational basis, led by parasiticides and osteoarthritis (OA) pain, while partially offset by lower sales of key dermatology products. Livestock revenue increased 8% on a reported basis and 9% on an organic operational basis, driven by growth in cattle and fish.

INNOVATION PIPELINE ADVANCEMENTS AND PORTFOLIO UPDATES
Zoetis continued to advance innovation across its diversified portfolio, and remains on track for expected 2026 product approvals, with select approvals anticipated in 2027 now expected in Q4 2026.

Regulatory Approvals and Portfolio Expansion
In October, Zoetis received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval for Convenia® RTU for Dogs, a long-acting antibacterial injection for dogs. This is the second major market approval this year, with both achieved ahead of the expected timelines, and expands Zoetis' portfolio of therapies designed to broaden treatment options for dogs. Convenia RTU for Dogs is expected to be available to veterinarians in the first quarter of 2027.

Zoetis launched Lenivia® for dogs in Great Britain and received Swiss approval for Portela® for cats, expanding access to long-acting osteoarthritis pain relief that can last up to three months with a single injection.

During the quarter, Simparica Trio® received U.S. FDA Emergency Use Authorization (EUA) for the treatment of New World screwworm infestations in dogs and puppies. The authorization adds to Zoetis' existing response, including Dectomax and Dectomax®-CA1 for other species at risk.

Additional approvals from the quarter include:

  • Fostera® PRRS Needle-free microdose was approved in the U.S. and Mexico to help protect pigs against porcine reproductive and respiratory syndrome.
  • Vanguard® B Oral received an additional claim in Brazil to help protect dogs against Bordetellabronchiseptica.
  • Vanguard® crLyme received an additional claim in Canada to help protect dogs against subclinical arthritis caused by Borrelia burgdorferi.
  • Alfaxan®Multidose4% was approved in Japan and Mexico for sedation of cats.

New Peer-Reviewed Research and Zoetis Medical Hub Launch

  • A peer-reviewed study published in Parasites & Vectors found that dogs treated with Simparica Trio had significantly lower average heartworm counts than dogs treated with NexGard ® Plus, with few dogs in the Simparica Trio group having adult heartworms at the study's conclusion.
  • The study is available through the newly launched Zoetis Medical Hub, which brings together medical expertise and peer-reviewed research.

FULL YEAR 2026 FINANCIAL GUIDANCE
Zoetis is providing updated guidance:

  • Narrowed revenue to a range of $9.220 billion to $9.320 billion (organic operational growth of (2)% to (1)%)
  • Raised adjusted net income to a range of $2.600 billion to $2.640 billion (organic operational growth of (7)% to (5)%)
  • Raised adjusted diluted EPS to a range of $6.25 to $6.35

This guidance reflects foreign exchange rates as of October 20, 2026. Additional details on guidance, including reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures and the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures, are included in the financial tables and will be discussed on the company's conference call.

About Zoetis
Zoetis is the world’s leading animal health company, driven by a singular purpose: to nurture our world and humankind by advancing care for animals. With a legacy of nearly 75 years, Zoetis continues to pioneer ways to predict, prevent, detect, and treat animal illness, supporting veterinarians, livestock producers, and pet owners in over 100 countries. We integrate deep scientific expertise, data-driven R&D, advanced manufacturing, and commercial excellence to deliver meaningful innovation across medicines, vaccines, diagnostics, biopharmaceuticals, and digital solutions. Guided by our vision to be the most trusted and valued animal health company, Zoetis is committed to setting new standards for the future of animal care through innovation, customer obsession, and purpose-driven colleagues. To learn more, visit Zoetis.com.
